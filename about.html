My story

I'm John, a graphic designer specializing in brand identity, logo design, and print design. I work with brands, churches, and community organizations to turn ideas into visuals people remember.

Much of my work is for church and community events, where a flyer has to be clear at a glance and look right both in print and on a phone screen. That habit carries into everything I design.

I take freelance projects and collaborations, and I'm always open to a new brief.
