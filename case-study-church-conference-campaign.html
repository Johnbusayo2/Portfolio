Visual identity and social media flyers for a church conference campaign.

The brief

A church conference needed a clear, recognizable look that would carry across its flyers and social media announcements.

My approach

I built one visual system, with consistent type, color, and layout, so every flyer reads as part of the same campaign whether it is printed or shared on a phone.
